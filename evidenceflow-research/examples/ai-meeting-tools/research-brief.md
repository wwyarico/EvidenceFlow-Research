# Example brief: AI meeting assistants

- Decision: Select one AI meeting assistant for a three-month pilot.
- Decision-maker: Product operations lead at a 20-person remote startup.
- Audience: Product, operations, and privacy stakeholders.
- Users/market: Bilingual Chinese-English remote team.
- Geography: Global availability; China-based team members.
- Time window: Current capabilities and pricing at collection date.
- Alternatives: Five tools shortlisted before detailed review.
- Criteria: Chinese/English transcription, summary usefulness, integrations, privacy controls, onboarding friction, and total cost.
- Non-goals: Proving population-wide accuracy or negotiating enterprise contracts.
- Deliverables: Evidence ledger, competitor matrix, recommendation memo, quality report.
- Human-review owner: Project researcher.

## Research questions

1. Which tools satisfy non-negotiable language and integration requirements?
2. What evidence supports advertised capabilities, and where is independent validation missing?
3. Which two tools should enter a hands-on pilot, and what metrics should determine the winner?

## Stopping rule

Stop when every criterion has a current primary source for each product or is explicitly marked unknown, and the two leading candidates have a documented pilot plan.
