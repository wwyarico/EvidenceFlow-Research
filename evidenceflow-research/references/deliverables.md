# Deliverables

## Decision memo

### Decision and scope

State the decision, owner, market, users, period, alternatives, and key limits.

### Executive summary

State what the evidence supports, why using claim IDs, and what uncertainty could change the answer.

### Findings

For every finding include the finding statement, supporting claim IDs, confidence, counterevidence or limitation, and decision implication.

### Recommendations

Each recommendation must have:

`action → intended outcome → supporting evidence → assumption → validation metric → stop/review condition`

Avoid universal recommendations when evidence supports only a segment or scenario.

### Method note

Record sources searched, inclusion rules, collection date, sample size, analysis method, AI assistance, human-review steps, and known gaps.

## One-page brief

Use five blocks: decision, evidence-backed answer, three key findings, risks and unknowns, and next action.

## Export package

When useful, deliver `research-brief.md`, `source-register.csv`, `evidence-ledger.csv`, `competitor-matrix.csv` or `voc-codebook.csv`, `decision-memo.md`, and `quality-report.md`.
